TRADITIONAL DRIP PERCOLATION

South Indian Filter

Iconic Indian Ritual

A beloved stainless steel or brass drip brewer that slowly yields a thick, bittersweet, and velvety "decoction" crafted to blend with hot, frothy boiled milk.

GRIND Fine / Chicory
RATIO 1:5 (30g / 150ml)
TIME 10 – 15 mins
TEMP 100°C (Boil)
💡 Pro Tip: Rest the pierced umbrella disc lightly on top of the grounds. Never pack or tamp tightly, which could choke the tiny perforations.

Step-by-Step Recipe

1

Assemble & Dose

Place 3 to 4 rounded tablespoons (approx. 25-30g) of dark-roasted coffee into the perforated top chamber.

2

Set the Pierced Disc

Place the pressing disc lightly over the grounds to disperse hot water evenly across the coffee bed.

3

Pour Boiling Water

Pour 120-150ml of freshly boiled water up to the brim. Fasten the lid securely to trap steam.

4

Decoct & Aerate

Allow 10-15 minutes for the thick decoction to collect. Blend 40ml decoction with 100ml hot frothy milk between a tumbler and davarah for signature froth.

Recommended roasts:
Blue Tokai (Attikan Estate Dark) ↗, Monsooned Malabar AA ↗

MANUAL POUR-OVER PERCOLATION

Hario V60 Pour-Over

Barista Choice

The gold standard of manual pour-over brewing. Spiral ribs and a conical design unlock full control over flow rate, highlighting delicate florals and tropical fruit notes.

GRIND Medium-Fine
RATIO 1:15 (15g / 225g)
TIME 2m 45s – 3m 15s
TEMP 91°C – 94°C
💡 Pro Tip: Always pre-rinse the paper filter with hot water to wash away paper taste and pre-heat the cone before dosing your coffee.

Step-by-Step Recipe

1

Rinse & Dose

Rinse the paper filter, discard water, and add 15g of ground coffee. Gently shake to level the bed.

2

The 45g Bloom

Pour 45g of water in a spiral. Let the coffee bubble for 45 seconds to release trapped CO₂.

3

Concentric Circle Pours

Pour steadily in concentric circles without washing down the paper sides. Reach 135g by 1:15, and the final 225g by 2:00.

4

Swirl & Draw-Down

Give a gentle swirl for a flat bed. Allow full draw-down by 2:45 to 3:00, swirl the carafe, and pour.

Recommended roasts:
Araku (Grand Reserve) ↗, KC Roasters (Kelagur Washed) ↗, Bloom Coffee (Ratnagiri Carbonic) ↗

HYBRID IMMERSION & PRESSURE

AeroPress Brewer

Versatile & Travel

Combines full immersion sweetness with micro-filtration and gentle pneumatic pressure. Forgiving, fast, and brews an intensely smooth cup in under 2 minutes.

GRIND Medium to Fine
RATIO 1:13 (15g / 200g)
TIME 1m 45s – 2m 00s
TEMP 85°C – 88°C
💡 Pro Tip: Stop pressing as soon as you hear a soft "hiss". Pushing air through the grounds extracts bitter, astringent compounds.

Step-by-Step Recipe (Inverted)

1

Inverted Setup

Insert the plunger 1cm into the chamber and stand upside down. Add 15g of ground coffee.

2

Pour & Agitate

Pour 200g of water (85°C-88°C). Stir 4-5 times with the paddle to ensure complete saturation.

3

Attach Cap & Flip

Rinse the paper filter in the cap and screw it on. At 1:30, carefully invert the AeroPress onto your cup.

4

Gentle Press

Press down slowly over 25-30 seconds until the first hiss. Pop the puck into the bin and enjoy.

Recommended roasts:
Corridor Seven (Riverdale N72) ↗, Subko (Project Sankalp) ↗, Third Wave (Baarbara Berry) ↗

FULL IMMERSION BREWING

French Press (Plunger)

Heavy Body

Pure full immersion with a reusable stainless mesh filter. Preserves natural coffee oils and fine aromatics for a velvety, creamy mouthfeel and deep chocolate finish.

GRIND Coarse (Kosher Salt)
RATIO 1:14 (20g / 280g)
TIME 4m 00s – 5m 00s
TEMP 94°C – 96°C
💡 Pro Tip: Do not leave remaining coffee sitting in the French press after plunging, or it will continue over-extracting. Pour all coffee immediately.

Step-by-Step Recipe

1

Dose & Fill

Preheat carafe with hot water and empty. Add 20g of coarse coffee, then vigorously pour 280ml hot water.

2

Rest & Steep

Set lid with plunger pulled up to keep heat trapped. Steep undisturbed for 4 minutes.

3

Break Crust & Skim

At 4 minutes, gently stir the crust 2 times so grounds sink. Skim floating white foam from the top.

4

Plunge Gently

Insert plunger and press down slowly over 15 seconds. Pour immediately into mugs.

Recommended roasts:
Blue Tokai (Attikan Dark) ↗, Third Wave (Baarbara Berry) ↗, Monsooned Malabar AA ↗

STOVETOP STEAM PRESSURE

Moka Pot (Stovetop)

Bold & Espresso-Style

The timeless Italian octagonal stovetop brewer. Steam pressure forces water up through the coffee funnel, producing a bold, syrupy concentrate ideal for milk brews or americanos.

GRIND Medium-Fine
RATIO Fill funnel level
TIME 3m – 5m on stove
WATER LEVEL Below safety valve
💡 Pro Tip: Use pre-heated hot water in the boiler base. This shortens stove time and prevents roasting the dry coffee grounds before water rises.

Step-by-Step Recipe

1

Preheated Water in Base

Fill the bottom boiler with hot water up to just below the brass safety valve.

2

Fill Funnel Basket

Fill the basket with medium-fine coffee. Level gently with your finger without tamping.

3

Gentle Low Heat

Tightly screw upper chamber on with a towel. Place over low-medium heat with lid open.

4

Cool & Serve

When a rich golden-hazel stream flows and begins gurgling, remove from heat and cool the base with cold water to stop extraction instantly.

Recommended roasts:
Araku (Grand Reserve) ↗, KC Roasters (Kelagur Washed) ↗, Blue Tokai (Attikan Dark) ↗

SLOW CHILLED EXTRACTION

Cold Brew Dripper / Steeper

Smooth & Low-Acid

Steeped in cold water over 14 to 18 hours. Avoids extracting bitter heat-soluble tannins, producing an ultra-smooth, chocolatey concentrate that keeps for up to 10 days.

GRIND Extra Coarse
RATIO 1:8 (50g / 400ml)
TIME 14 – 18 hrs (Chilled)
SHELF LIFE 10 Days in fridge
💡 Pro Tip: Mix 1 part cold brew concentrate with 1 part tonic water and an orange peel for a refreshing summer coffee spritz.

Step-by-Step Recipe

1

Coarse Dose & Cold Water

Grind 50g beans extra coarse. Place in a jar or steeper and add 400ml cold filtered water.

2

Gentle Stir & Seal

Gently stir to immerse all dry grounds completely. Seal tightly with lid.

3

Overnight Steep

Store in refrigerator to steep undisturbed for 14 to 16 hours.

4

Filter & Serve

Strain through fine mesh or paper filter. Serve over ice diluted 1:1 with water or milk.

Recommended roasts:
Maverick & Farmer (Parama Cold-Smoked) ↗, Subko (Project Sankalp) ↗
